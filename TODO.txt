- Fix Garmin export: total distance reported is weird
   - Repro: a 1250-yard workout shows up in Garmin as 3750 yards.
   - `estimatedDistanceInMeters` in src/garminExport.ts is set from
     calcTotalDistance(workout), which matches the intended total — so
     Garmin is likely recomputing from the step structure and getting
     a 3x figure.
   - Suspects:
       a) The nested per-step RepeatGroupDTO (step.repetitions > 1) may
          be counted in addition to the executable step rather than
          containing it.
       b) All single-rep executable steps in a set share the same
          childStepId (the set's). If Garmin treats childStepId as a
          grouping key, this could multiply the count.
       c) estimatedDistanceInMeters / estimatedDistanceUnit shape may be
          wrong, causing Garmin to ignore it and recompute.
   - Strong lead: the inflation appears tied to the lap-button rest
     steps we now emit between steps and after each set. The rest
     ExecutableStepDTOs may be getting counted as distance even though
     their endCondition is lap.button (not distance) — possibly because
     they reuse `strokeType`/`equipmentType` fields or are missing a
     flag that excludes them from distance totals. Try: omit the rest
     steps entirely and confirm the total matches; then reintroduce
     them with a corrected shape.

- Garmin-side bug (unit handling on round-trip) — NOT fixable from our
  code, file as a complaint to Garmin:
   - Workout X is really 2500 yd.
   - Original (sourced from MySwimPro) on Garmin Connect: website shows
     2286 yd; watch shows 2500 yd correctly.
   - After downloading from Garmin Connect and re-uploading the same
     file: website shows 2500 yd; watch now shows 2734 yd.
   - Diffed before (old.json) vs. after (new.json). Per-step distances
     are byte-identical. Only meaningful payload change:
       estimatedDistanceInMeters: 2286 -> 2500
     i.e. Garmin converted m -> yd (2286 * 1.0936 = 2500) and wrote the
     yd value back into the meters-named field on re-upload. The watch
     then reads it as meters again -> 2500 m -> 2734 yd display.
   - Side observations during the round-trip (also Garmin's doing):
       * consumer / consumerName / workoutProvider get nulled out.
       * Description em-dashes get mojibaked (UTF-8 reinterpreted as
         Latin-1: "—" -> "â").
       * stepIds get reassigned.
   - Our code is not in the loop for this round-trip; do NOT try to
     "fix" it in garminExport.ts.
